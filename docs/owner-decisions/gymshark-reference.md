On 03.10.2026 the owner wrote about https://www.gymshark.com: «хороший магазин, берём за пример, а можем даже как пример для последующей витрины использовать». In the same talk he chose the shelf head «угол» (И678) and pointed at Gymshark's text width: «они примерно 70% ширины для текста используют».

Measured that day at 1440: category intro text («Leggings») 798px wide, 14px, ~127 chars per line — 68 % of the column in the owner's 1253 CSS-px window; section captions on the home page 400px under the heading, left; product carousels — heading left with a «view all» link right after it, prev/next arrows 44px in the top right corner on the heading line. Our caption measure followed it: `--measure-lede` 73ch ≈ 800px (И680).

**How to apply:** first-tier reference next to [[allbirds-reference]] in design briefs (docs/design/, И474): measure it before deciding layouts of heads, captions, rails and category pages. When a new storefront starts, offer Gymshark as the model to measure from. Related: [[measure-before-styling]], [[pro-practice-default]].
