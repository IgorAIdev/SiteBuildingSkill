The storefront template has no buyers. It is a prepared shop front that the owner views himself. Owner, 29.09.2026: «да нет никаких покупателей сейчас, это всё заготовка витрины».

**Why:** texts like «покупатели увидят после публикации» and «черновик видите только вы» describe a live shop that doesn't exist. They confuse the owner.

**How to apply:** in the design-system page and the look panel, word it as «сохранить вид» / «закрепить за витриной». Keep the draft mechanism itself; just don't mention buyers or visitors. In texts about the site's UX (breadcrumbs, cart), «покупатель» still means the future shop's buyer and is fine. Related: [[template-sample-content]], [[look-panel-architecture]].
