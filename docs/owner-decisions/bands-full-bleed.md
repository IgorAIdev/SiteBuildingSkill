03.10.2026, after a drawn comparison on a 49-inch (5120 px) window, the owner answered: «делай как у Shopify, GOV.UK, Stripe и IKEA: фон до края окна, содержимое в рамке». That is what the site already does (band across the whole window, frame about 1392 px centred, hero inside the frame, header bar full width), so nothing was changed.

**Why:** the pros' sources (Dawn `page-width`, Horizon section background layer, GOV.UK, Stripe, IKEA) all do it; the «band only as wide as the frame» variant exists at one shop in nine (Cibdol).

**How to apply:** don't box the band to the frame and don't re-ask. Wider frames (Horizon 1920 / 2400) were offered and not requested; the panel Layout → Width stays 1440 / 1280 / 1600. Evidence and numbers: `docs/sources-harvest.md` §7, `.claude/skills/craft/references/layout.md`. Related: [[effects-row-stays-tiles]].
