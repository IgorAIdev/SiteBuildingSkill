When a control or sign can lose contrast (heart over a dark product photo), the answer is a palette role measured by the builder (glass `--plate-glass`, `--pop-on-glass`, И606) — not a question to the owner and not paint on the place. Owner 01.10.2026: «ты идиот, у нас палитра для чего? чтоб таких проблем не было».

**Why:** the kit's palette exists exactly to guarantee contrast on every ground; asking reopens a solved layer.

**How to apply:** place names the fact (`data-over="picture"`), the control's module takes the role; no role yet → extend tools/palette.mjs with a measured role and a check. See [[only-correct-architecture]], [[no-unasked-extras]].
