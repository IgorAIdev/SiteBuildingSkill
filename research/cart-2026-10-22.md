# Cart page and cart drawer — anatomy of the best stores (research 2026-10-22/23)

Purpose: concrete, sourced numbers for the redesign of the storefront cart page and
cart drawer. Every number below names its source. "Measured" = read from the live DOM
with `getBoundingClientRect()` / `getComputedStyle()` in a browser tab at **1280×860**
(desktop) and **390×844** (phone) CSS px. "CSS" = read from theme source. "Computed" =
derived arithmetically from CSS values (stated as such). Anything not verified is marked
**not verified**.

## 1. Short answers to the questions

**Does the store even have a cart page?** Only 2 of the 6 measured live stores do
(GS, RH). AB, GL, EV, CB are drawer-only: `/cart` redirects to the home page and/or
opens the drawer (AB → `/?openCartDrawer=true`; EV → `/`; CB → `/`; GL `/cart` renders
an empty `main` and the drawer `aside#bag` open). Dawn's default `cart_type` is
`"notification"` (popup), Horizon's default is `"drawer"` (`config/settings_data.json`:
Dawn L175, Horizon L55).

**Cart page, desktop — two columns?** 3 of 4 cart pages: H, GS, RH. Dawn does **not** —
its summary is a separate section *below* the lines, a right-aligned 350px block
(`component-cart.css` L134–151: `.cart__footer {display:flex; justify-content:space-between}`,
`.cart__footer > * {width:35rem}`; L72–74 `> div:only-child {margin-left:auto}`).

**Summary width:** GS 335px content (375px column incl. 40px right padding, measured),
RH 358px (measured), H `min(50vw, 25rem)` = 400px (≥750px; `main-cart.liquid` L105, fixed
400px ≥1400px L133), Dawn 350px block (L144–146). Gap lines↔summary: H 3rem = 48px
(L68), RH 80px (measured grid `704px 358px`, gap 80px), GS ≈32px (measured x 858→890).

**Summary as a card?** 1 of 4: RH (bg `rgb(132,130,126)`, radius 12px, padding 20px,
white text — measured). H: default `extend_summary: true`, `border: none`, no background
(`templates/cart.json`; `blocks/_cart-summary.liquid` schema defaults) → a bare column that
the merchant *can* colour as a full-height side panel; GS and Dawn bare.

**Sticky summary?** 1 of 4: H (`.cart-summary__inner {position: sticky; top: 0}`,
`blocks/_cart-summary.liquid` L46–47; `top: var(--header-height)` when header sticky,
L58–60). GS, RH, Dawn: `position: static` (measured / CSS).

**Line item — thumbnail.** Cart page desktop: Dawn 100px (`.cart-item__media {width:10rem}`
L578–580), H 120px (`7.5rem` column when the items container ≥720px, `cart-products.liquid`
L783–788), GS 69×86 (measured), RH ≈74×93 (measured). Phone: Dawn ≈79px (computed:
4-col grid, gap 15px, in 360px; cap `max-width: calc(10rem/scale)` = 100px, L47–50,
L498–503), H `clamp(2.5rem, 15cqi, 7.5rem)` ≈ 54px at 358px container (computed, L489),
GS 69×86 (measured), RH 66×100 (measured).

**Quantity — own column or under the name?** Cart page desktop: own column in Dawn (table
column, `padding-left: 5rem` L606–611), H (≥720px container: `'media details quantity
price'`, L788–792), GS (stepper at right end of the line row, x 738–826, measured). Under
the name: RH. Phone: under the name in all 4. Drawer: never its own column (8 of 8) —
5 of 8 put it in the text column under the name (Dawn, H, GL, EV, RH), CB in a row under
the thumbnail, AB and GS bottom-right under the price.

**Remove.** Cart page: trash icon next to the stepper — Dawn (`icon-remove.svg`, 15×15
icon in a 35×35 button ≥750 / 45×45 <750, L395–421), H (`icon-delete.svg`, 44×44 button,
`cart-products.liquid` L695–703). No separate remove control (minus at 1 removes) — GS,
RH. Drawer: text link "Remove" 3/8 (AB under the variant; GL underlined at right of the
stepper row; EV underlined next to the stepper), trash icon 3/8 (Dawn, H, CB 36×36 at the
top-right), none 2/8 (GS, RH).

**Unit price when qty = 1.** Dawn and H always print the unit price inside the text
column *and* the line total in the price column (Dawn `main-cart-items.liquid` L139–141
`item.original_price`; H `cart-products.liquid` L303–336). All 6 live stores print a
single price per line.

**Line total position.** Right column, top-aligned: Dawn, H, RH (page); in drawers 7 of 8
(price top-right). GS is the exception: price under the variant, left (page and drawer).

**Vertical rhythm inside a line.** Name→variant gap: H 2px (`--margin-3xs: .125rem`,
L623–626), GS 2px, RH 2px, CB 2px, EV 3px, Dawn 6px (`.cart-item__details > * + *
{margin-top:.6rem}` L68–70; option→option 4px L470–472), AB 6px, GL 11px (all measured
except Dawn/H). Name size 12–18px: AB 12/500 uppercase, EV 12 uppercase, GS 14/400, GL 14,
CB 14/600, Dawn 15 (`h4`), H 16, RH 18/700 uppercase. Variant 12–14px grey (AB #575757,
GS #9FA3A8, GL #666, EV #737373). Stepper usually aligned to the bottom of the thumbnail
(EV, GS, Dawn drawer row 2).

**Row spacing and separators.** Hairline between lines: H (1px `rgb(fg / .10–.25)`, 24px
padding + 24px margin; `templates/cart.json` `gap: 24, dividers: true`, L607–610), RH
(0.8px dotted #DDDEDC, padding 16px/8px), CB (border-top gray-100, 12px + 12px). No line:
Dawn (rows separated by 40px `td {padding-top:4rem}` L557–560; phone 35px margin L502;
one 1px border after the last row, `component-cart.css` L58–62), AB (12px margin), GL
(12px padding top+bottom), GS. Per-line cards: 0 of 8.

**Summary contents — order** (cart page):
- Dawn: cart-level discounts (12px) → "Estimated total" 16px + value 18px → tax note
  "Taxes, discounts and shipping calculated at checkout." → "Check out" (full width, max
  360px, min-height 45px) → dynamic checkout (express) buttons, max 360px
  (`main-cart-footer.liquid` L49–124; `component-cart.css` L154–193; `component-totals.css`).
- H: [Subtotal + discount lines — only when a cart discount exists] → "Discount" accordion
  (on by default) [+ note accordion if enabled] between hairlines → "Estimated total" 14px
  label + bold value (cart `xl`, 18px on phone) → [installments] → tax note (subdued) →
  "Check out" full width, height 52px (computed: `clamp(25px, 3.25rem, 55px)`, L477) →
  accelerated checkout buttons stacked vertically, 10px gap (L470). 32px gap between
  totals and buttons (`_cart-summary.liquid` L43). Then a "You may also like" 4-product
  section (`templates/cart.json`).
- GS: "Summary" → "Order summary": Sub Total / Estimated Shipping $5 / **Total** → "Checkout
  securely" (335×51, blue) → 8 payment marks 40×24 → "Pay in 4 interest-free payments of
  $9.00 · Learn more" → "Also available at checkout: Klarna, Afterpay, Sezzle" → "Free
  Standard Delivery for orders over $…", "Express Delivery Available" → "Need help?"
  (Delivery Information, Make A Return). Left column also carries "Your items aren't
  reserved…", a free-shipping progress bar and a "Delivery options" disclosure (Standard
  $5, 3–6 working days, free over $75; Express $15, 3 working days).
- RH: "1 item" → "add $25.00 more for FREE shipping" (+ bar) → subtotal → "*shipping,
  taxes, and discounts calculated at checkout." → CHECKOUT (white, 303×49).

**Drawer.** Width: Dawn 400 (`width:40rem; max-width: calc(100vw - 3rem)`,
`component-cart-drawer.css` L18–30), GL 435 (420 content), H 480 (`--theme-drawer-width:
30rem`, `theme-styles-variables.liquid` L135; `max-width:100%`), EV 480, GS 500, CB 500
(`max-w-[500px]`), AB 531 (`max-w-[531px]`), RH 680 (max 708). **Median ≈ 490px; 7 of 8
sit in 400–531px.** Phone: full width in AB, GL, H, CB (measured / CSS); Dawn leaves a
30px strip (360px at 390). Side padding: AB 10, GL 12, Dawn 15, EV 16, H 20
(`--theme-drawer-padding: 1.25rem`), CB 20, GS 24, RH 32 — median ≈18px. Thumbnail: CB
56×56, H ≈66 (computed `15cqi` of 440px), GS 69×86, RH 74×≈100, AB 85×85, Dawn ≈85
(computed `repeat(4,1fr)` in 370px, gap 10px), GL 97×121, EV 160×200 — median ≈80px.
Pinned foot with the checkout button: **8 of 8** (fixed/sticky, or a flex column whose
item list scrolls). Foot contents: see section 3.

**Phone (390px) cart page.** All 4 cart pages go single-column, lines first, summary
after; none of the 4 makes the summary or checkout button sticky on the page (GS checkout
358×51 at y≈859 in flow; RH 326×46 in flow; Dawn/H in flow). Dawn centres totals and tax
note below 750px (`component-totals.css` L1–5, `component-cart.css` L185–189).

## 5. Baymard Institute — guidelines and figures (public pages)

- **Abandonment.** Average documented cart abandonment **70.22%** (50 studies).
  Reasons, US shoppers (Baymard survey; "just browsing" 42% excluded): extra costs too
  high **40%**, delivery too slow **20%**, didn't trust site with card **19%**, forced
  account **18%**, checkout too long **17%**, errors **17%**, returns policy **13%**,
  couldn't see total cost up front **12%**, card declined **10%**, not enough payment
  methods **9%**. — https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate (fetched 2026-10-22).
  Other Baymard articles round differently: 39% extra costs, 19% forced account, 21%
  slow delivery (https://baymard.com/blog/reduce-cart-abandonment, 2026-05-19); 14%
  couldn't see total (https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization).
- **Full cost in the cart.** Show the full order cost in the cart incl. fees and per-item
  costs, or a clear estimate with how it was calculated; put free-shipping qualification
  in the order summary; check that ads/cross-sells don't dominate the cart; allow "save
  for later"; persist the cart across sessions. — https://baymard.com/blog/audit-checkout-flow-hidden-friction
  (2026-05-13); https://baymard.com/blog/reduce-cart-abandonment. Guideline #732
  "Essential Price Info for the Cart" is rated *Essential* (text paywalled) —
  https://baymard.com/guidelines/732-essential-price-info-for-the-cart.
- **Coupon field.** "3) Hide 'Coupon Code' (35% Don't)": 70% of sites have a promo
  field; **35% of sites with one display it by default**; hide it behind a link; Adidas
  keeps it collapsed in the cart. — https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
  (2024-06-26). Apply qualifying discounts automatically and highlight a code that
  changes the price; an "Apply" button is acceptable for promo/gift codes. —
  https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-usability-apply-buttons (updated 2025-05-14).
- **Quantity / remove.** Use +/− buttons, or buttons with a text field; 61% of sites use
  only a text field or only a drop-down (2022-09-20); auto-update (≈200–300ms delay for
  typed values), update totals immediately; minus at qty 1 should remove; honour "0";
  offer undo after removal. — https://baymard.com/blog/auto-update-users-quantity-changes.
  The 2025 roundup lists the same guideline as "(97% Don't)" —
  https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux (2024-11-13, upd. 2025-11-25);
  the two figures come from different benchmark rounds and were not reconciled.
- **Cross-sells in the cart.** 52% of desktop sites show cross-sells in the cart or
  "added to cart" step that are irrelevant or based only on "others bought". Six fixes:
  dynamic count instead of a fixed number; be cautious with alternatives (complements
  first); label the reason ("Frequently bought together", "Complete the look"); same use
  case/theme; compatibility-dependent items first; promos only when they fit the order
  size. No placement rule (above/below) is given. —
  https://baymard.com/research-articles/product-recommendations-cart (2021-01-12).
- **Save for later.** Guideline #622: users may delete items they want later and lose
  them; advice text paywalled. — https://baymard.com/guidelines/622-implementing-a-cart-save-for-later-feature.
- **Delivery.** "Use 'Delivery Date' instead of 'Delivery Speed' (48% Don't)"; show
  delivery dates/shipping speeds early and keep shipping info visible in the cart. —
  https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux; https://baymard.com/blog/reduce-cart-abandonment.
- **Trust and payment.** 19% abandoned because they didn't trust the site with card
  details (n = 1,026, 2025); in the 2023 seal study Norton ranked first; "having some
  visible seal or icon matters more than which one"; seals and padlocks work best next to
  the card fields, which should be visually encapsulated (findings are about the payment
  step, not the cart). — https://baymard.com/research-articles/perceived-security-of-payment-form.
  Show accepted payment methods "on the cart or checkout entry page". —
  https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization.
- **Guest checkout.** 62% of sites don't make "Guest" the most prominent option. —
  https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux.
- **Continue shopping / product attributes in cart.** No public Baymard cart guideline
  text found. Nearest public statements: the cart should confirm item, variation and
  quantity and link back to the product page (guideline #2160 "Linking from the Cart to
  Product Pages", text paywalled); the checkout should keep a clear way back to the shop
  (https://baymard.com/blog/reduce-cart-abandonment). Treat as **not verified**.
