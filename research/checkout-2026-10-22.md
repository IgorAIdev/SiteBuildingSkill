# Checkout anatomy — research 22–23.10.2026

Purpose: guide the redesign of the kit's 3-step checkout (Contact → Delivery → Payment).

## Method

- **Shopify Checkout was measured live, not taken from screenshots.** Six Shopify stores were opened through Shopify cart permalinks (`/cart/<variantId>:1`, a plain GET that opens checkout). Nothing was typed and no form was submitted. Geometry, computed styles and the shell's CSS custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle` at 1253, 1440 and 1920 px wide, at 999 px (below the breakpoint) and at 390 px (phone).
  - One-page checkouts: Allbirds US (allbirds.com), Allbirds UK (allbirds.co.uk), Glossier (glossier.com), Death Wish Coffee (deathwishcoffee.com), Shopify's own Hydrogen demo store (hydrogen-preview.myshopify.com → checkout.hydrogen.shop), SNOCKS DE (snocks.com).
  - Three-page checkout (`/information` step): Beardbrand (beardbrand.com).
  - Checkout session URLs expire, so the store domain stands in as the citation for measured facts. Every store runs the same Shopify build: the class names and CSS variables were identical across all of them.
- **Stripe was not measured.** The browser refused to open buy.stripe.com. Stripe facts below come from Stripe docs plus one third-party help page.
- **Baymard** facts come from its public articles; its guidelines and benchmark pages are paywalled.
- **Apple, Nike and Zalando** have no public layout documentation. What exists is listed and marked weak.

## 1. Shopify Checkout — measured anatomy

### 1.1 Desktop shell (≥ 1000 px viewport)

These values were read from the shell element `.cm5pp` at 1440 px. The root font-size is 10px, so 1rem = 10px. Values were identical in all six stores.

```
--x-shell-content-template-columns: minmax(min-content, calc(50% + calc(calc(58rem - 48rem) / 2))) 1fr
--x-shell-content-template-areas:   "main order-summary"
--x-shell-main-inline-size:         58rem      (580px, includes 40px padding each side → 500px content)
--x-shell-main-justify-content:     flex-end   (main column hugs the split line)
--x-shell-main-padding:             4rem       (40px)
--x-shell-order-summary-inline-size:48rem      (480px, includes 40px padding → 400px content)
--x-shell-order-summary-padding:    4rem
--x-shell-header-inline-size:       calc(58rem + 48rem) = 1060px
--x-shell-header-padding:           2rem 4rem  (full-width header variant)
--x-shell-inline-size:              calc(58rem + 48rem)
--x-spacing-base: 1.4rem · large-200: 2rem · large-300: 2.4rem · large-600: 4.8rem
order summary wrapper: position: sticky
```

How the split works:

- **Column formula:** the left grid column is `50% + 50px`; the right column takes the rest (`1fr`). The split line therefore sits 50px right of the viewport centre.
- **Main column:** its 580px wrapper is pushed against the split line (`justify-content: flex-end`). The summary's 480px wrapper starts at the split line.
- **Centring:** the two wrappers form one 1060px block whose edges sit at `50% − 530px` and `50% + 530px`. **The block is exactly centred on the viewport at every width.**
- **Content and gap:** content is 500px (form) and 400px (summary), a 5:4 ratio. The gap between form and summary content is 80px (40 + 40).
- **Your estimate:** you expected about 560–600 px main and 420–450 px summary. The real figures are 500/400 content, or 580/480 including padding.

Measured columns (the content width excludes the scrollbar):

| Window | Content width | Grid columns | Form content x | Summary content x | Grey panel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1253 | 1238 | 669.2 / 569.2 | 129–629 | 709–1109 | x 669 → 1238 (window edge) |
| 1440 | 1425 | 762 / 663 | 223–723 | 803–1203 | x 763 → 1425 |
| 1920 | 1905 | 1002.4 / 902.4 | 462–962 | 1042–1442 | x 1002 → 1905 |

Source: live measurement at allbirds.com, glossier.com, checkout.hydrogen.shop, deathwishcoffee.com and beardbrand.com.

**Grey summary panel:** default `#f5f5f5` (rgb 245,245,245), measured at Allbirds and the Hydrogen demo. Merchants recolour it (Death Wish uses rgb 249,247,247). It fills the whole right grid cell, out to the window edge and down to the page bottom. Help Center: the order summary background colour or image is a merchant setting (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/checkout-extensibility/checkout-branding).

**Header: two variants, chosen by the merchant.** The header position setting has three values: `START`, `INLINE` and `INLINE_SECONDARY` (https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/enums/CheckoutBrandingHeaderPosition). The Help Center names one of them "Full width" (branding URL above).

- **Full-width header** (shell class `U3Rye`; Allbirds, Death Wish, Hydrogen demo):
  - The white bar spans the window, with a 1px bottom divider (Allbirds: rgb 222,222,222).
  - Its inner box is 1060px wide with 20px/40px padding, so the logo lines up with the form's left edge and the cart icon with the summary's right edge.
  - The cart icon is "shown by default in one-page checkout" (https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/CheckoutBrandingHeader).
  - **The grey panel starts below the header:** at y = 66 on the Hydrogen demo, 100 at Allbirds and 191 at Death Wish.
  - Measured header heights: 66px (text logo at 20px/600), 100px (60px logo), 191px (150px logo).
- **Inline header** (shell class `_5ZebG`; Glossier, SNOCKS, Beardbrand):
  - The logo sits inside the main column, aligned with the form: Glossier logo at x = 222, y = 40.
  - Left or centred alignment is a setting; Beardbrand centres the logo.
  - **The grey panel runs from y = 0, the very top of the page, beside the logo.**
  - The Beardbrand three-page checkout uses this variant: logo, then breadcrumbs, both in the main column.
- **Which variant is the default:** not verified. Shopify's own Hydrogen demo, the closest thing to defaults, uses full width. The cart-icon doc implies full width is the one-page default.

### 1.2 Breakpoints

- **Live checkout:** the two columns switch at `min-width: 1000px` (shell CSS rules). At a 999 px window the page is one column, centred, with a 580px max wrapper and 20px/14px padding, so content is up to 552px. The order-summary toggle becomes a full-width 63px grey bar under the header.
- **Legacy checkout.liquid stylesheet:** it had four breakpoints, 0 / 750 / 1000 / 1300 px (https://shopify.dev/themes/architecture/layouts/checkout-liquid/checkout-css, deprecated).
- **Kit mapping:** the kit's nearest seam to Shopify's 1000 is 1080.

### 1.3 One-page vs three-page (2023+)

- **One-page is the default layout.** Since 16.11.2023 merchants on every plan can switch between one-page and three-page checkout (https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/all-merchants-can-now-choose-between-the-one-page-checkout-and-three-page-checkout-1).
- **How each one is structured:** three-page splits checkout into information, shipping and payment pages "that buyers progress through in sequence". One-page puts everything "on a single scrollable page alongside the order summary" (https://shopify.dev/docs/api/checkout-ui-extensions/latest/targets/page-layouts).
- **What one-page adds:** with prefilled buyer data it collapses finished sections into "rollups", meaning summaries of what was entered (same doc).
- **What each one shows at the top:** one-page has no step indicator. Three-page has text breadcrumbs.

### 1.4 Main column top to bottom — contact / information step

**Three-page, Information step** (Beardbrand at 1440; y in px from page top):

1. Logo (inline header) at y = 46–134.
2. Breadcrumbs `Cart › Information › Shipping › Payment` at y = 173.
3. H1 "Information", **visually hidden** (1×1 px).
4. "Express checkout" heading (centred, 16px), wallet buttons 48px tall, then an "OR" divider (y = 258–381).
5. H2 "Contact" with a **"Sign in" link on the same row, right-aligned** (y = 442).
6. Email field, then the "Email me with news and offers" checkbox.
7. H2 "Shipping address": Country select · First name | Last name (2 columns) · Company (optional) · Address · Apartment, suite, etc. (optional) · City | State | ZIP (3 columns) · Phone.
8. Button row (y = 1558): **primary "Continue to shipping" on the right, "‹ Return to cart" text link on the left.**
9. Footer: "Terms and policies".

**One-page** (Hydrogen demo and Allbirds; Hydrogen y at 1440):

1. Full-width header bar.
2. Visually hidden H1 "<Store> Checkout".
3. "Express checkout" (14px, centred over the form) with wallet buttons (y = 106), then "OR".
4. H2 "Contact" + "Sign in" link right (y = 246), then the Email field.
   - Allbirds adds the helper "Used for your order confirmation and cart reminders"; whether that is Shopify's default text is not verified.
   - Then the marketing opt-in checkbox.
5. H2 "Delivery" (y = 394): country, names, address fields.
   - Allbirds UK puts the phone helper "In case we need to contact you about your order" under the Phone field.
6. H2 "Shipping method" (y = 741): placeholder text "Enter your shipping address to view available shipping methods."
7. H2 "Payment" with the line "All transactions are secure and encrypted.", then a bordered radio list: card fields, Shop Pay, PayPal, and so on. Below it, the "Use shipping address as billing address" checkbox, which is ticked by default.
8. "Pay now <total>" button, full width (y = 1275).
9. "Terms and policies" (y = 1372).

Spacing inside the main column:

- Section starts are about 148px apart (Contact → Delivery).
- The footer sits 48px below the last block (`margin-top: var(--x-spacing-large-600)`).

**Fields (Shopify):**

- **Labels are floating:** the label sits inside the field at the top, as measured at Beardbrand, where the label is at x 258 inside a field starting at x 246.
- **Size:** Shopify defaults are 14px text and 47px-tall fields (Hydrogen demo, Allbirds). Beardbrand sets 16px text and 55px fields.
- **Paired fields:** First | Last and City | State | ZIP share a row.

### 1.5 Step indicator

- **Three-page uses text breadcrumbs, not numbered circles.** Order: `Cart › Information › Shipping › Payment`, separated by a 14×14 `chevronRight` SVG.
  - "Cart" is a link back to the cart.
  - **The current step is `<strong>`.**
  - Steps not yet reached are plain spans and cannot be clicked.
  - Font size follows the base size: 16px at Beardbrand (custom base). Shopify's default size is not verified.
  - On a phone the breadcrumbs sit **below** the order-summary bar (Beardbrand at 390, y = 1062).
- **One-page has no indicator at all.**
- **Baymard:** "the progress indicator must be a 1:1 map of the checkout process", and past steps should be clickable (https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization). The problem it addresses: "Users can become disoriented without an accurate indication of how long their current process is" (https://baymard.com/guidelines/611-checkout-steps-and-corresponding-process-steps; full advice paywalled).
- **Numbered circles:** none of the measured leaders use them. Yoast notes that Nike shows where the shopper is "without a formal progress bar" (https://yoast.com/checkout-page-ux/).

### 1.6 Page title — is the step name repeated?

- **No visible duplicate.** In three-page the step name appears visibly once: the bold breadcrumb item. The page `<h1>` "Information" exists but is visually hidden (1×1 px, 32px font), for screen readers only.
- **One-page:** the H1 "<Store> Checkout" is also visually hidden (Hydrogen, Allbirds, Death Wish).
  - On Glossier and Death Wish the visible H1 is the logo or store name inside the header.
- **What is visible:** section `<h2>` headings (Contact, Delivery / Shipping address, Shipping method, Payment) at 18–22px.
  - Measured sizes: Hydrogen 20px/600 (Shipping method 16px/600); Allbirds 18px; Glossier 22px/700.

### 1.7 Buttons

- **Three-page (Beardbrand):**
  - Row `display:flex; flex-direction:row-reverse; justify-content:space-between`. **In the DOM the primary button comes first**, so it is first in keyboard order and on top when stacked.
  - The primary button is content-width (200×49 px, "Continue to shipping") and sits on the right.
  - The secondary is a text link with a 14px chevron-left icon, "Return to cart", on the left of the same row.
- **One-page:** "Pay now $X" is **full main-column width**, 500×50 px, with the total inside the label.
  - Measured at Hydrogen, Allbirds and Death Wish; Glossier's "Place Order" is 500×47.
  - The Hydrogen demo button is blue rgb(0,91,209), radius 12px, 14px/600. Whether these are Shopify defaults is not verified.
- **Phone (three-page):** the primary is full width (326×49) with "Return to cart" full width directly below it (Beardbrand at 390).
- **Phone (one-page):** "Pay now" is full width, 362×50 at 390.

### 1.8 Footer

- **Present and minimal.** In the 2026 build it is a single "Terms and policies" button (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, 14px) that opens a dialog of policies. It sits at the foot of the main column, 48px below the form, under the form.
  - Measured at Hydrogen, Glossier, Death Wish and Beardbrand. Allbirds adds its own line with terms and privacy links (12px).
  - Beardbrand's variant (`BwmDa`) is a full-width footer bar.
- **No site navigation:** out-of-the-box Shopify does not let merchants add the full site header (https://outofthesandbox.com/blogs/shopify-theme-blog/why-the-shopify-checkout-is-the-way-it-is).
- **Older Shopify:** a visible row of individual policy links. Not verified on today's build.

### 1.9 Order summary anatomy

Measured on Allbirds UK, Beardbrand and SNOCKS:

- **Visually hidden headings:** "Order summary" (H2), plus H3 "Shopping cart", H3 "Discount code or gift card" and H3 "Cost summary".
- **Line item:**
  - Thumbnail **64×64**, with a placeholder background (#ededed) and a 2px white border at Allbirds UK; corner radius is a store setting (0/12/16 px seen).
  - **Quantity badge** 20×20 px, 12px bold, dark background, radius 8px, overlapping the thumbnail's top-right corner by about 4px.
  - Product name 14px; variant 12px, muted (text at 56% opacity); line price 14px, right-aligned.
  - SNOCKS and Beardbrand use 13–16px base sizes.
  - Long carts scroll inside the box ("Scroll for more items").
- **Discount:** the field and an "Apply" button share one row (field 331px, Apply 61×35 at Allbirds). On a phone the discount field is collapsed by default (Help Center, branding URL above).
- **Cost summary:**
  - "Subtotal · 2 items".
  - "Shipping": muted "Enter shipping address" (one-page) or "Calculated at next step" (three-page).
  - **"Total"** 18–20px bold, with the **currency code** before the amount in 12px muted ("GBP", "USD", "EUR").
  - EU/UK stores add **"Including £21.16 in taxes"** in 14px under the total (allbirds.co.uk). SNOCKS replaces this with its own text, "inkl. MwSt. & Versand".
- **Desktop behaviour:** the summary wrapper is `position: sticky`.

### 1.10 Phone layout (390 px)

One-page, Hydrogen demo and Allbirds:

- **Header:** 66px; store name or logo on the left with a 14px gutter, cart icon on the right.
- **Then a full-bleed grey bar, 63px:**
  - Left side: "Order summary" plus a chevron, 14px.
  - Right side: the total, 18px bold.
  - It is collapsed by default (`aria-expanded="false"`; Help Center: collapsed by default). It is not sticky.
- **Then** Express checkout, Contact, Delivery, Shipping method, Payment, full-width "Pay now", Terms and policies.
- **Page side padding:** 14px (`--x-spacing-base`).

Three-page phone (Beardbrand):

- The summary bar sits under the header; Beardbrand's opened expanded, which is a store setting.
- The breadcrumbs come **after** the summary, then the form.
- The primary button is above a full-width "Return to cart".

## 2. Stripe Checkout (hosted)

- **Branding settings** (https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout/customization/appearance.md?ui=full-page): logo or icon (full hosted page only), background colour, button colour, font family, border style (for example `rectangular`), and display name.
- **Split layout:** the order or offer summary is on the **left** and the payment details (contact, method, card) are on the **right**. Source: a third-party help page (https://support.okast.tv/en/support/solutions/articles/103000268167-set-up-the-stripe-payment-page-checkout-for-your-users).
- **Not verified:** which side the background colour fills, the column widths and max-widths, and the mobile behaviour. The browser refused to open buy.stripe.com, so nothing was measured.

## 3. Baymard Institute — public guidance with numbers

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Steps / fields | Average checkout for a new user is **5.1 steps**; **11.3 form fields** in 2024, down from 12.7 in 2019; most sites need only **8 fields** | https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields |
| Reduce fields | Single name field; hide Address line 2 and the coupon field behind links; billing defaults to shipping; account creation offered on the confirmation step | same |
| Single column | "use a single primary column to display form fields"; 16% of sites use multi-column forms | https://baymard.com/blog/form-design |
| Labels | "Position labels above fields, so they remain visible once users have started typing"; placeholder text "doesn't replace the need for a clear label" | https://baymard.com/blog/form-design |
| Labels on mobile | Labels above the field by default; left-aligned only in landscape | https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-form-usability-label-position |
| Inline validation | Validate on blur, not while the user types; once an error shows, update it on each keystroke; positive validation helps; 31–32% of sites have none | https://baymard.com/blog/inline-form-validation |
| Required / optional | Mark **both** (asterisk + "(optional)" next to the label, not as placeholder); only 14% of sites do this on desktop and 6% on mobile | https://baymard.com/blog/required-optional-form-fields |
| Same, 2025 | 61% don't mark both; 32% of test users skipped a required field when only optional fields were marked | https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux |
| Guest checkout | 62% of sites fail to make "Guest Checkout" the most prominent option; put it at the top as a high-contrast button, not a text link; 18–19% abandon because they don't want an account | https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization ; https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux |
| Button wording | Replace generic "Continue" with "Continue to Payment"; final button "Place Order", the most prominent element | https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization |
| Order summary | Checklist: "Does the order summary remain visible throughout the flow?" | same |
| Trust near payment | Visually encapsulate the card fields (border or background, distinct from other fields); 1–2 icons inside that area; seals close to the card fields; 19% abandoned because they didn't trust the site with card info (2025) | https://baymard.com/blog/perceived-security-of-payment-form |
| Delivery date | Show a delivery **date** or date range, not shipping speed; 41% of sites don't (2023); 48% (2025) | https://baymard.com/blog/shipping-speed-vs-delivery-date ; https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux |
| Phone field | "sites should clearly explain why a phone number is required and what it will be used for"; 39% don't; explanation inline next to the field; example wording "In case we need to contact you about your order" | https://baymard.com/blog/explain-phone-number-field |
| Errors | Specific messages; keep all input after an error; scroll to the error, or show a summary when there are several | https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization |
| Abandonment | 70.22% average; extra costs too high 40%; delivery too slow 20%; didn't trust site with card 19%; +35.26% conversion possible from checkout design | https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate |

- **Labels — Shopify vs Baymard:** Shopify's floating in-field labels differ from Baymard's "label above the field".
- **Paired fields:** Shopify's First|Last and City|State|ZIP rows are the kind of exception Baymard allows for name and address fields (https://baymard.com/learn/form-design).
- **"How we use your data" link:** no public Baymard source was found for this pattern. The nearest documented ones are Baymard's phone-field explanation and Shopify's helper text under the field.

## 4. Apple / Nike / Zalando

- **Apple** (2022 teardown, https://shoprocket.io/blog/teardown-of-apples-ecommerce-experience): many separate pages, including a sign-in gate with "Continue as guest", a single-question delivery page, then address, payment and a review page with "Place your order" at the bottom. Column layout and headings are not documented (not verified).
- **Nike:** Nike's help pages say the estimated delivery date is shown during checkout (https://www.nike.com/hr/help/a/shipping-delivery). Yoast: no formal progress bar (https://yoast.com/checkout-page-ux/).
- **Zalando:** only paywalled Baymard benchmark screenshots exist (e.g. https://baymard.com/checkout-usability/benchmark/step-type/payment/9182-zalando). Nothing verifiable on layout.

## 5. Direct answers

1. **Desktop proportions.** Shopify's grid is `minmax(min-content, 50% + 50px) 1fr`.
   - Content is 500px (form) | 400px (summary) with an 80px gap. Including padding, the boxes are 580 | 480 = 1060px.
   - The block is centred on the viewport at every width; grey fills from the split line to the window edge.
   - With a full-width header, the grey starts **below** the header. With an inline header (logo in the main column, as in the three-page Beardbrand and in Glossier), it starts **at the very top** of the page, beside the logo.
2. **Main column on the contact step.** Logo, breadcrumbs, express wallets + "OR", "Contact" + "Sign in" on one row, email, opt-in, address block, buttons ("‹ Return to cart" left, primary right), "Terms and policies". The one-page version uses a full-width "Pay now" at the end instead.
3. **Step indicator.** Text breadcrumbs (Cart › Information › Shipping › Payment) with chevrons: the current step bold, Cart a link, future steps not clickable. The leaders don't use numbered circles. Baymard requires a 1:1 map with clickable past steps. One-page has no indicator.
4. **Title repetition.** No. The step name shows once (the bold breadcrumb). The H1 is visually hidden. Visible headings are section H2s.
5. **Buttons.** Three-page: content-width primary (about 200×49) on the right, "Return to cart" text link with chevron on the left, same row, primary first in the DOM (`row-reverse`). One-page: full-width 500×50 "Pay now <total>". Phone: full width, with the link below.
6. **Footer.** Present, one quiet line: "Terms and policies" (opens a dialog), 48px under the form, no site navigation.
7. **Phone.** 66px header (logo left, cart icon right), a 63px grey "Order summary ⌄ … total" bar collapsed by default, a single 14px-gutter column, a full-width primary button, then the policies line.
