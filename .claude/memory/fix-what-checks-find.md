Whatever a check I ran shows red, or whatever debt a new check finds, I fix in the same turn — I don't report it as «another session's», «known debt, recorded as baseline» or «say the word and I'll do it». Owner 01.10.2026: «нахуй ты мне провёл проверки и не исправлял их? исправляй» and «нет других сессий сейчас рабочих».

**Why:** the owner isn't technical; a list of red checks is work handed back to him. Blaming a parallel session without checking was simply wrong that day.

**How to apply:** before reporting, every check I touched is green; a new ratchet starts at its honest count only after the real fixes are done. Don't assume other sessions exist — ask `ListAgents` or look at file times only if it matters. Related: [[site-audit-unasked]], [[take-recommended]].
