Reference points for design, layout and structure are the best examples (top stores of any niche — Ritual, Seed, Cibdol, Charlotte's Web, Endoca, Glossier, Aesop…), not local market competitors. Said by the owner on 13.10.2026, angrily: «при чём тут болгарские конкуренты, я разве говорил их смотреть… ориентироваться нужно на лучшие примеры».

**Why:** a weak local competitor shows what the market currently does, not what works; copying or even measuring against it drags the standard down. The owner never asked for it — I had offered «посмотреть магазины вашего рынка» as a check and he said «делай» without taking it as an instruction to benchmark locally.

**How to apply:** when researching structure/design, read the best-in-class pages (verify what loads; some block fetches, say so). Local competitors only when the owner names them, and then for facts (prices, assortment, SERP), not as a standard. Don't offer them as a step. Market-specific demand and SERP data (what people search, who ranks) is a different question and stays as in the SEO research. Related: [[measure-before-styling]], [[known-norms-answer-directly]].
