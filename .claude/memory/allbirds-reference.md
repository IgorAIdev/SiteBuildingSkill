On 29.09.2026 the owner pointed at https://www.allbirds.com/ («там кнопки да и дизайн в целом хороший минималистичный») and asked how many button kinds it has («мне кажется одна»).

Measured the same day at 1440 (home, /collections/mens, /products/mens-tree-runners): one button form everywhere — full pill, 12px, weight 500, uppercase, tracking 0.3px, text only, no icons. Two heights, 33 and 49 (buy row). Only the colour treatment changes with the floor: dark fill #212121 with white label on light, white fill with dark label on photos, and an outline (white on photos, dark for prev/next). Size picks are a separate selector (2px corners, grey hairline). The one outlier was a 6px-corner «CONFIRM» in a country modal.

**How to apply:** use it as a first-tier reference in design briefs (docs/design/, И474) for buttons and minimal layouts. Button kinds follow its model: one form, the fill says the importance. Related: [[design-with-skills]], [[measure-before-styling]].
