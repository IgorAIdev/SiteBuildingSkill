On 03.10.2026 the owner, angry about a broken shelf head, wrote «переделывай, предложи варианты тут в чате». I applied the recommended variant on the storefront at once (the broken state did not wait) and drew three variants in the chat as one inline mockup: the real heading «CBD oils», the real caption text, Manrope, the site's proportions of heading, caption and 40px buttons, a clipped product rail under each; the recommended one marked «рекомендую · уже на витрине», each with a one-click «Беру B ↗» button. He answered within minutes: «да давай вар. А» plus a refinement.

**Why:** the kit rule «выбор показывается глазами, а не списком» and his «no screenshots» preference meet here: a drawn mockup in the chat is seen without a screenshot, and three options with one recommended is what he can decide on (many variants annoyed him before — «ты много полос сделал… выбери те, что по стилистике»).

**How to apply:** for layout choices asked in chat — fix the broken state with the recommended variant first, then show ≤3 variants drawn with real content; numbers and reasons go in the text, not in the drawing. Related: [[take-recommended]], [[no-screenshots-batch-prs]].
