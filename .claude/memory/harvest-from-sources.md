Owner 03.10.2026, while I researched how pros break up a monotonous home page: «ты ж можешь из них и ещё что-то полезное ценное почерпнуть, что нам в будущем понадобится».

**Why:** each research round opens expensive primary sources (Baymard, NN/g, Shopify Dawn/Horizon code). Answering only the asked question throws the rest away, and the next surface repeats the same reading.

**How to apply:** after answering the question, in the same round take a second pass over the same sources for other pages and stages (catalogue, product page, cart, section catalogue of Horizon, spacing/type numbers, colour-scheme roles, accessibility). Record each fact as «источник (URL) · факт или число · где пригодится · есть ли у нас» in the repo (`docs/sources-harvest.md`, linked from `docs/references.md`), mark what was not opened at the primary source, and don't turn a finding into a rule without an implementation. Keep it bounded: pages already opened are not reopened. Related: [[pro-practice-default]], [[measure-before-styling]].
