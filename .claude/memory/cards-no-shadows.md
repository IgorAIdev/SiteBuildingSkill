Product cards stay without shadows; the Shadows knob (Look → Shape → Shadows) is not wired to them. Owner, 04.10.2026, asked whether Shadows should reach the cards: «тени у карточек не нужны». Same line as 30.09.2026 («при не наведении какая-то тень есть — убирай её»).

**Why:** the owner's card is flat (Framed: line at rest, darker line under the hand); shadows live on menus, panes and windows only.

**How to apply:** don't connect `--sh-raised` / `--sh-lift` to card designs and don't offer it again; the Shape tab hint already says cards stay flat. Exception kept from 30.09: Sheet and Edged designs lift under the hand — leave unless the owner asks. Related: [[panel-knob-reaches-all]], [[round-forms-only]].
